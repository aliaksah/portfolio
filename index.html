Aliaksandr Hubin

Associate Professor in Statistics • Norwegian University of Life Sciences (NMBU)
Associate Professor in Statistics • University of Oslo (UiO)

I am an Associate Professor in Statistics and Data Science based in Norway. My research is situated at the intersection of Bayesian statistics, probabilistic machine learning, and computational statistics.

My primary methodological focus includes scalable Bayesian model selection and model averaging (BMA), efficient Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) algorithms, Variational Bayes, and sparse explainable deep learning through Latent Binary Bayesian Neural Networks (LBBNN). I actively apply these frameworks to complex problems in genomics, biostatistics, sports medicine, and operations research.

Key Research Areas
Bayesian Statistics Model Selection & Averaging (BMA) Bayesian Deep Learning (LBBNN) MCMC & Mode Jumping Algorithms Variational Inference Explainable AI & Sparsity Biostatistics
